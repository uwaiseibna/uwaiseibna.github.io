Enrolled as a Computer and Information Science PhD student in University of Florida. I work under the supervision of Dr. Christina Boucher with research concentration in Bionformatics. Currently working on scalable PBWT querying on BioBank scale data on phased haplotypes. Previously, I worked as a research assistant in the AssisGroup at Florida Atlantic University where I worked on developing ML framework to learn cross-species genotype-phenotype associations from gaps in sequence alignments. I like to play soccer and travel when I am not busy with work.
